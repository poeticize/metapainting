Love and Burning

Heat, Heart, and Collective Flames

Creation Layers & Reverberation

1. Idea Sketch

Original background composition before masking

Love and Burning captures the intensity of collective emotion through heat, fire, and heart symbolism. Beginning in early 2023, over 50 participants contributed individual brushstrokes to the flame canvas—turning a solitary vision into a radiant fire of community expression.
