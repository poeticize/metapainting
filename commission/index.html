Why Metapainting

Art That Brings People Together

A Metapainting isn't just a painting — it's a layered experience. It begins with a completed work of art, which is then masked and divided into numbered sections for your participants to claim, peel, and paint. The result is a permanent record of a shared moment — artist and community fused on one surface, every participant a valid creator, inseparable and unique.

Perfect For

Corporate Events

Team-building through shared creation. Nothing bonds a group like making something beautiful together.

Festivals & Fairs

Interactive art activation that engages thousands of attendees over the course of an event.

Community & Nonprofits

A physical monument to a community's shared vision, values, or milestone.

Private Celebrations

Weddings, anniversaries, or family reunions — create an heirloom painted by everyone who attended.
